# Flowglass art prompts

The app ships original **vector** creatures (`src/components/art/Critter.tsx`) and logo
(`FlowglassMark.tsx`). Use these prompts to get **painted** versions for marketing, the
store listing or a future art upgrade. Keep each family looking like the *same creature
growing up*: same eyes, same proportions, more detail each stage.

**Rules for every prompt**
- Original character. Do not reference, imitate or name existing games, anime, brands,
  celebrities or their catchphrases (no "in the style of …", no real faces).
- Check the generator's license allows commercial use before shipping anything.
- Generate on a plain background so the art can be cut out and placed on the app's panels.

**Shared style block** (append to every prompt):
> polished indie-game character art, chibi proportions, soft cel shading with subtle
> rim light, clean silhouette readable at small sizes, warm gold and midnight-blue palette
> with one family accent color, plain light-grey background, centered, full body, no text

## The five stages (all families)

| Stage | Species | Body | Feel |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Grain | tiny golden sand-grain teardrop, big round eyes, stubby feet | harmless, slightly confused |
| 2 | Pebble | rounded beige pebble, small green sprout on top, blush cheeks | sturdier, more confident |
| 3 | Rock | faceted blue-grey stone with stubby arms, teal crystals on the head, thick eyebrows | personality, attitude |
| 4 | Monolith | tall dark stone golem with big shoulders, glowing gold sand cracks, glowing eyes | imposing, legendary-in-progress |
| 5 | Timekeeper | hooded sand mage (see below) | the mascot |

## The Timekeeper (mascot + logo)

> An original mysterious sand mage mascot: midnight-blue hood with two swept-back points,
> thin gold trim and a gold diamond gem on the forehead; the face is hidden in shadow with
> two glowing warm-yellow eyes; flowing dark-blue cape; holds an ornate gold hourglass with
> glowing amber sand at its side; a few floating gold sand grains around it. Confident,
> calm, a little mischievous. Palette: midnight blue, warm gold, a touch of teal.

**App icon variant:**
> Only the Timekeeper's hooded head, front view, centered on a rounded-square midnight-blue
> tile: hood with gold trim and diamond gem, shadowed face with two glowing yellow eyes, one
> gold sand grain falling below. Flat, bold shapes, readable at 32×32 px, no text.
